## Asosiy g'oya

**will** — eng sodda kelajak shakli: hamma shaxs uchun bir xil, -s yo'q.

<Callout tone="tip">
Og'zaki nutqda deyarli doim qisqa shakl: I'll, you'll, she'll, it'll. Inkor: **won't** [wount].
</Callout>
