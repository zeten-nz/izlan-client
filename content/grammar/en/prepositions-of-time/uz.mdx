## Asosiy g'oya

Uchburchak sifatida eslang: **at** — eng kichik (nuqta), **on** — o'rtacha (kun), **in** — eng katta (oy, yil, fasl).

<Callout tone="tip">
Amerikancha talaffuzni tanlaganimiz uchun **on the weekend** deymiz.
</Callout>
