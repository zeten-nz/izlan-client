## Asosiy g'oya

Savol: qaror **qachon** qabul qilingan? Hozir — **will**. Oldin — **going to**. Kalendarga yozilgan — **Present Continuous**.

<Callout tone="info">
Har bir shakl alohida sahifada batafsil: Future Simple, be going to, Present Continuous.
</Callout>
