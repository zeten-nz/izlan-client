## Qachon ishlatiladi

- **Hozir, shu daqiqada:** now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
- **Vaqtinchalik:** this week, these days.
- **Aniq reja (kelajak):** I'm flying to Moscow on Friday.

<Callout tone="tip">
O'zbekchadagi **-yap** qo'shimchasi ko'pincha Present Continuous ga mos keladi: o'qi**yap**man — I'm reading.
</Callout>
