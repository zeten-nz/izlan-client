## Asosiy g'oya

Hozirgi zamonda am/is/are bo'lsa, o'tganda faqat ikkita shakl: **was** (birlik, I bilan) va **were** (ko'plik va you bilan).

<Callout tone="warning">
To be bilan **did** ishlatilmaydi: Were you…? (not: Did you be…?).
</Callout>
