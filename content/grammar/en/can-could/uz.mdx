## Asosiy g'oya

**can** — o'zbekchadagi «**-a olaman**»: suza olaman — I can swim.

<Callout tone="tip">
Iltimosda **could** muloyimroq: Could you help me? Notanish odamga shunday murojaat qiling.
</Callout>
