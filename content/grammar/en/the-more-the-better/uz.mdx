## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **«qancha …, shuncha …»** = **The … , the …**

<Callout tone="tip">
Qisqa iboralar: *The sooner, the better.* (Qancha tez bo'lsa, shuncha yaxshi.) *The more, the merrier.*
</Callout>
