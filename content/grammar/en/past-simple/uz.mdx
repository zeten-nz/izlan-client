## Asosiy g'oya

Past Simple — o'tmishda **tugagan**, vaqti ma'lum ish. O'zbekchadagi **-di** (bordim, ko'rdi) ga mos.

Muhim: Past Simple'da **hamma shaxs uchun shakl bir xil** — I went, she went, they went.

<Callout tone="tip">
Noto'g'ri fe'llarni yodlash kerak: alohida «Noto'g'ri fe'llar» jadvaliga qarang.
</Callout>
