## Asosiy g'oya

**wish** bilan fe'l **bir zamon orqaga** suriladi: hozir → Past Simple, o'tmish → Past Perfect.

<Callout tone="tip">
O'zbekchadagi «**-saydim / qani endi**»: Bilsaydim — I wish I knew.
</Callout>
