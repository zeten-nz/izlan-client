## Asosiy g'oya

**might** — «balki», «… -sa kerak»: Balki kelar — He might come.

<Callout tone="tip">
maybe (balki) — ravish, gap boshida: **Maybe** he'll come. may be — fe'l birikmasi: He **may be** late.
</Callout>
