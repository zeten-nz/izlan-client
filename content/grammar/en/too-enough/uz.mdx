## Asosiy g'oya

**too** — «haddan tashqari», odatda natija salbiy. **enough** — «yetarli».

<Callout tone="tip">
enough ning joyi: **sifatdan keyin**, **otdan oldin** — old enough, enough time.
</Callout>
