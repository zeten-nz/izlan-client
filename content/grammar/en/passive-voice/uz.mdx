## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **-il / -in** qo'shimchasi: qur**il**di — was built, yoz**il**gan — was written.

<Callout tone="tip">
V3 (3-shakl) kerak: noto'g'ri fe'llar jadvalidagi uchinchi ustun — built, written, made, stolen.
</Callout>
