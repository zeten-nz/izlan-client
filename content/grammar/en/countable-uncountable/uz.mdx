## Asosiy g'oya

Savol bering: **bittalab sanash mumkinmi?** Bir olma, ikki olma — mumkin. Bir suv, ikki suv — yo'q (bir **stakan** suv deyiladi).

Sanalmaydigan narsani o'lchov bilan sanaymiz: **a glass of** water, **a cup of** tea, **a piece of** advice.

<Callout tone="tip">
money ham sanalmaydi: some money, much money. Lekin dollars, coins — sanaladi.
</Callout>
