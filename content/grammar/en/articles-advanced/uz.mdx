## Asosiy g'oya

| the bilan | Artiklsiz |
|---|---|
| daryolar, dengizlar, okeanlar | shaharlar, ko'pchilik davlatlar |
| tog' **tizmalari** (the Pamirs) | bitta tog' (Mount Elbrus) |
| the USA, the UK, the Philippines | qit'alar (Asia, Africa) |
| cho'llar (the Kyzylkum) | ko'llar (Lake Baikal) |

<Callout tone="info">
**school, hospital, prison, church, bed, work** — o'z vazifasi uchun borilsa artiklsiz: *go to bed, be in hospital*. Bino sifatida — *the*.
</Callout>
