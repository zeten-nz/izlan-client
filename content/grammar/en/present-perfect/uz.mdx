## Qachon ishlatiladi

Present Perfect o'tgan voqeani **hozirgi payt bilan bog'laydi**. Muhim narsa — voqea qachon bo'lgani emas, hozirgi natija yoki tajriba.

- **Tajriba:** I have visited Samarkand twice.
- **Hozirgina tugagan ish:** We have just finished the lesson.
- **Hozirgacha davom etayotgan holat:** She has lived in Tashkent since 2020.

<Callout tone="warning">
Aniq o'tgan vaqt (yesterday, last year, in 2019) aytilsa, Past Simple ishlating.
</Callout>

## Tez-tez uchraydigan so'zlar

ever, never, just, already, yet, since, for, so far, recently.
