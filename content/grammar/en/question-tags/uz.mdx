## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **«-ku», «shunday emasmi?»**: Sovuq-**ku**? — It's cold, isn't it?

<Callout tone="tip">
Tag dagi olmosh gap egasiga mos: **Ali** is late, isn't **he**?
</Callout>
