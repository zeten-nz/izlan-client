## Asosiy g'oya

Qoida yo'q, **fe'lga bog'liq**. Eng yaxshi usul — fe'lni doim qolipi bilan yodlash: «enjoy **doing**», «decide **to do**».

<Callout tone="tip">
Predlogdan keyin har doim **-ing**: interested in learning, good at swimming, before leaving.
</Callout>
