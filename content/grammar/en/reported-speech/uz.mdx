## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchada ko'chirma gapda zamon o'zgarmaydi: «U **charchaganini** aytdi». Inglizchada esa asosiy fe'l o'tgan zamonda bo'lsa (said), ichki gap ham orqaga suriladi.

<Callout tone="tip">
that ni og'zaki nutqda tushirish mumkin: He said (that) he was busy.
</Callout>
