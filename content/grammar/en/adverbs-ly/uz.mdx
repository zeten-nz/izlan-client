## Asosiy g'oya

Savol bering: **kim/nima qanday?** — sifat. **Qanday bajaradi?** — ravish.

<Callout tone="tip">
O'zbekchada ko'pincha bir xil so'z: «tez mashina» / «tez haydaydi». Inglizchada: a fast car / drives fast — lekin quick / quickly.
</Callout>
