## Asosiy g'oya

Javobni tasavvur qiling: **Ali** called me — Ali ega → **Who called** you? I called **Ali** — Ali to'ldiruvchi → **Who did** you call?

<Callout tone="tip">
«What happened?» — eng ko'p ishlatiladigan ega haqidagi savol. Hech qachon «What did happen?» emas.
</Callout>
