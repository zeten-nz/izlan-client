## Qachon ishlatiladi

First Conditional **real, bo'lishi mumkin** bo'lgan kelajak vaziyatlari uchun: shart bajarilsa, natija ham bo'ladi.

<Callout tone="warning">
if-qismida will ishlatmang: "If it rains" to'g'ri, "If it will rain" xato.
</Callout>

## Bog'liq mavzu

Present Perfect ham if-qismida kelishi mumkin: If you have finished, we will go.
