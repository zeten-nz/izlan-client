## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **«-ardim / -ar edim»** ga mos: o'ynardim — I used to play.

<Callout tone="tip">
Faqat o'tmish uchun. Hozirgi odat — Present Simple: I usually play.
</Callout>
