## Asosiy g'oya

Bu so'zlar some/any qoidasiga bo'ysunadi. O'zbekchada «hech kim **kelmadi**» (ikki inkor) normal, inglizchada esa bitta inkor: Nobody **came**.

<Callout tone="warning">
I don't know nothing — xato. To'g'risi: I don't know anything yoki I know nothing.
</Callout>
