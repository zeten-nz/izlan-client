## Asosiy g'oya

Formula doim: **modal + have + V3**. Ma'no modalga bog'liq: should — afsus, could — imkoniyat, must / can't — xulosa.

<Callout tone="tip">
O'zbekchadagi «**-shim kerak edi**» — should have, «**-gan bo'lsa kerak**» — must have.
</Callout>
