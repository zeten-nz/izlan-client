## Asosiy g'oya

Past Continuous — o'tmishdagi **fon** (sahna orqasi), Past Simple esa o'sha fondagi **voqea**:

I **was walking** home (fon) **when** I **saw** Ali (voqea).

<Callout tone="tip">
O'zbekchadagi «-ayotgan edi» ga mos: ketayotgan edim — I was going.
</Callout>
