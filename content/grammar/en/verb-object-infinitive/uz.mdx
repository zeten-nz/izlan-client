## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchada «kelishingni xohlayman» deymiz. Inglizchada **kishi oldin, harakat keyin** keladi: *I want **you** **to come***.

| Fe'l | Misol |
|---|---|
| want | I want **him to win**. |
| ask | She asked **me to wait**. |
| tell | They told **us to sit** down. |
| expect | We expect **you to finish** today. |
| remind | Remind **me to call** her. |

<Callout tone="warning">
Kishi olmoshi **object** shaklda: me, you, him, her, us, them (I / he emas).
</Callout>
