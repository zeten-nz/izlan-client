## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **«go'yo»**, **«xuddi …dek»**, **«...ga o'xshaydi»**.

<Callout tone="info">
Shakl ma'noni bildiradi: *as if he **is** ill* — kasal bo'lishi mumkin; *as if he **were** ill* — aslida kasal emas.
</Callout>
