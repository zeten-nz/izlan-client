## Asosiy g'oya

Frazali fe'llar og'zaki nutqda juda ko'p. Ularni **butun ibora** sifatida yodlang, alohida so'zlarga bo'lmang.

<Callout tone="tip">
Olmosh (it, them, him) bo'lsa — doim o'rtaga: put **it** on, turn **them** off.
</Callout>
