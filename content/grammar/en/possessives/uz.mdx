## Asosiy g'oya

- **my, your, his…** — doim **ot bilan**: my book, her phone.
- **mine, yours, hers…** — **otsiz**, otning o'rnini bosadi: The book is mine.
- **'s** — biror odam yoki hayvonga tegishlilik: Ali's book, the cat's bowl.

<Callout tone="warning">
Egalik olmoshlarida apostrof yo'q: yours, hers, ours, theirs, its. «your's», «her's» — xato.
</Callout>
