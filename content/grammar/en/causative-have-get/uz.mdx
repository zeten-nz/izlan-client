## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekchadagi **-tir / -dir** (qildir, tuzattir): sochimni **oldirdim** — I had my hair cut.

<Callout tone="tip">
So'z tartibi muhim: I had **my car** repaired (boshqa odam tuzatdi) ≠ I had repaired my car (o'zim tuzatgan edim).
</Callout>
