## Qachon ishlatiladi

- **Odat va takror:** every day, usually, often, on Mondays.
- **Doimiy holat:** I live in Tashkent. She works as a doctor.
- **Umumiy haqiqat:** The sun rises in the east.
- **Jadval:** The film starts at 8.

<Callout tone="warning">
Eng ko'p xato — he/she/it bilan -s ni unutish: She work**s**. Inkor va so'roqda esa -s **does**ga o'tadi: She doesn't work. Does she work?
</Callout>
