## Asosiy g'oya

Eng muhim farq — **keyin nima keladi**:

| + gap (ega + fe'l) | + ot |
|---|---|
| because, as, since | because of, due to, thanks to |

<Callout tone="tip">
**thanks to** — ijobiy sabab: *Thanks to your help, I passed.*
</Callout>
