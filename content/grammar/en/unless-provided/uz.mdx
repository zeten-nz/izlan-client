## Asosiy g'oya

**unless** = if … not. **as long as** = faqat shu shart bilan. **in case** = ehtiyot uchun.

<Callout tone="tip">
Bu bog'lovchilardan keyin ham kelajak uchun Present Simple: unless you **hurry** (not: will hurry).
</Callout>
