## Asosiy g'oya

Joy predloglari (in, on) — **qayerda?** savoliga javob. Harakat predloglari — **qayerga? qayerdan? qaysi yo'l bilan?**

<Callout tone="tip">
Yo'l tushuntirishda juda kerak: go **along** this street, turn left, go **past** the school.
</Callout>
