## Asosiy g'oya

Zero conditional — **qonuniyat**: shart bo'lsa, natija **har doim** shu.

<Callout tone="tip">
Agar «if» ni «har safar ... bo'lganda» deb almashtirsa ma'no buzilmasa — bu zero conditional.
</Callout>
