## Asosiy g'oya

Ikkalasi ham «xohlayman», farqi — **odob darajasida**. Notanish odam, kafe, do'kon bilan **I'd like** ishlating.

<Callout tone="info">
I'd like = I would like. Talaffuz: [ayd layk].
</Callout>
