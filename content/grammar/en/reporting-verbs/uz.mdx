## Asosiy g'oya

Ko'chirma gapda faqat **said / told** emas, **ma'noni** beradigan fe'l tanlang. Har bir fe'lning tuzilmasini birga yodlang.

| Fe'l | Tuzilma |
|---|---|
| offer, refuse, promise | + to do |
| warn, advise, remind | + kishi + to do |
| deny, admit, suggest | + doing |
| apologise | + for doing |
