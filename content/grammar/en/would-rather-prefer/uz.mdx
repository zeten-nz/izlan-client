## Asosiy g'oya

- **would rather** — «...ganim ma'qul»: hozir yoki ma'lum bir holatda tanlov.
- **prefer** — umuman nimani yoqtirishingiz.

<Callout tone="warning">
**'d** — ham *would*, ham *had* qisqartmasi. *I'd rather* = I would rather; *I'd better* = I had better.
</Callout>
