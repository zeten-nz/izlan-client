## Asosiy g'oya

**was supposed to** = «...ishi kerak edi (lekin bo'lmadi)». Ko'p uchraydigan og'zaki ibora.

<Callout tone="warning">
Talaffuz: *supposed to* [səˈpoʊs tə] — **d** deyarli eshitilmaydi, lekin yozuvda shart.
</Callout>
