## Asosiy g'oya

O'zbekcha «U shifokor» gapida fe'l ko'rinmaydi. Inglizchada esa gapda **doim fe'l bo'lishi kerak**: She **is** a doctor.

To be quyidagilar uchun ishlatiladi: kim/nima ekan (a doctor), qanday ekan (happy), qayerda (at home), yosh (15), qayerdan (from Tashkent).

<Callout tone="tip">
O'tgan zamonda: was / were — alohida qoidaga qarang.
</Callout>
