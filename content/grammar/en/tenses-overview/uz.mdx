## Jadvalni qanday o'qish kerak

- **Qator** — vaqt: hozir (Present), o'tgan (Past), kelasi (Future).
- **Ustun** — ko'rinish (aspekt): voqeaga qanday qarayapmiz.

Har bir ustunning o'z «yasovchisi» bor:

- **Continuous** — doim **be + -ing**;
- **Perfect** — doim **have + V3** (fe'lning 3-shakli);
- **Perfect Continuous** — **have been + -ing**.

<Callout tone="tip">
Hammasini birdaniga yodlash shart emas. Avval Present Simple, Present Continuous va Past Simple ni puxta o'rganing — kundalik nutqning katta qismi shu uchtasida.
</Callout>
